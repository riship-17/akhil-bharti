About

One day, the whole higher-education community of Gujarat

The State Conference brings together teachers and principals from colleges and universities in every district of Gujarat, from both the college and university cadres.

It is a chance for the academic community to meet in one place, hear from the organisation, and discuss the role of the teacher in higher education today. Details of the programme will be shared by the organising committee.

Akhil Bharatiya Rashtriya Shaikshik Mahasangh (ABRSM) is a nationwide organisation of teachers working across school and higher education. The Gujarat unit's Higher Education wing hosts this conference.

Registration

Four steps, about five minutes

  1. Open the registration form

    Click Register on this page. The form opens on Google Forms — sign in with any Google account when asked.

  2. Fill in your details

    Name, contact number, district, cadre, designation, institution and residential address.

  3. Pay ₹500 by UPI

    Scan the QR code shown in the form with GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or BHIM. Take a screenshot once the payment succeeds.

  4. Upload proof and submit

    Enter the UTR / transaction number and upload the screenshot. The organising team verifies each payment and then sends your confirmation.

FAQ

Common questions

Who can register?
Teachers and principals of colleges and universities in Gujarat — Principals, Professors, Associate and Assistant Professors, and Teaching Assistants, from both college and university cadres.
How do I pay the ₹500 fee?
By UPI. The QR code and payment details appear inside the registration form. Pay with any UPI app, then upload the screenshot in the same form.
Why does the form ask me to sign in to Google?
Google Forms requires sign-in for any form that accepts a file upload — here, your payment screenshot. Any Gmail or Google account will work.
Where do I find the UTR number?
Open the payment in your UPI app's history. It is listed as UPI Ref No., UTR or Transaction ID — usually a 12-digit number.
I paid, but the form didn't submit. What should I do?
Do not pay again. Open the form once more, fill it in, and use the same UTR number and screenshot from your first payment.
Can I register on behalf of colleagues?
Please submit a separate form for each participant, with that person's own details and payment proof, so every registration can be verified and confirmed individually.
When will I receive my confirmation?
After the organising team has matched your payment with the bank statement. You don't need to do anything further once the form is submitted.
